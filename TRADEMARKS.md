- **Referring to the project:** "I use geenii", "a tutorial for geenii",

  endorsed by fm-labs.
- **Stating origin:** "based on geenii" or "a fork of geenii" in the

- **Community use:** blog posts, talks, articles, educational material and

- Offering geenii as a hosted or managed service under the geenii name.

You are free to fork geenii under the terms of the GPL. If you distribute a
modified version, give it a different name and logo, and remove or replace the
Marks in the user interface, documentation and package metadata. You may state
that your project is "based on geenii".
